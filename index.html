01 / About Me

I am a PhD researcher in the Faculty of Education at the University of Macau. My research focuses on computational thinking (CT), collaborative learning, and science/STEM education in K–12 settings.

I examine both learning processes and outcomes, with attention to instructional design and students’ interactions during computational problem solving. My work combines quantitative and qualitative methods.
